Garden Grove neighborhoods and the SR-22 corridor

Garden Grove runs along State Route 22, the Garden Grove Freeway, and its map has several panhandles. West Garden Grove lies west of Beach Boulevard and is largely separated from the rest of the city by Stanton. A southern panhandle borders Fountain Valley, which is why many of our Garden Grove calls start with a short trip across the city line.

That layout matters on a bad day. Give dispatch your cross streets and the freeway side you are on so a crew does not lose time. Our water mitigation team then starts extraction and drying as soon as it arrives.

Who owns the pipe? Sewer backups in Garden Grove

The Garden Grove Sanitary District handles sewer utilities, operated by the city's Water Services Division of Public Works, and the Orange County Sanitation District treats the wastewater. Property owners maintain, repair and clean the private sewer lateral between the home and the public sewer. For a public sewer overflow or spill, residents call the Public Works Main Office.

So if sewage is coming up through a floor drain, shut off water use, keep everyone away and call Garden Grove Public Works if the main is overflowing. Then call us for the part inside your walls. Our water damage cleanup work removes contaminated material and sanitizes what can be saved.

Mid-century growth, aging systems

Garden Grove incorporated in 1956, and its population jumped from 5,762 in 1950 to 84,238 in 1960. Pacific Electric arrived in 1905, and the 1933 Long Beach earthquake destroyed much of the central business district. Most of the housing stock grew in the postwar surge, so many homes are now past the 60-year mark.

In practical terms, original plumbing, water heaters and roof sections wear out. In the 2020 census the city had 48,562 housing units, 54.0 percent owner-occupied. Whether you own or rent, ask your landlord or inspector how old your supply lines and heater are before a leak asks the question for you.

Mold after a leak: when to call before it spreads

Mold needs moisture, so the leak must be fixed first. Remediation then follows the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it.

Festivals, Little Seoul and nearby towns

The Garden Grove Strawberry Festival has run each Memorial Day weekend since 1958, and the Little Seoul business district is a busy commercial corner. For shops and restaurants, a leak means lost business hours, so tell dispatch your opening time and which rooms are critical.

Crews arrive with extraction units, air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and moisture meters, and the written scope is explained before work starts. A quick call also helps us tell you whether the job is drying only or needs removal of wet drywall and flooring.
