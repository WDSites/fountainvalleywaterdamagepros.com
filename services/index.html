How the Services Fit Together

Most jobs start with emergency water removal when water is standing, then move to water mitigation so walls and floors dry out. After that comes water damage cleanup for contaminated or soaked materials and water damage repair for the rebuild.

Flood and sewage losses add flood damage restoration, which brings extra safety steps and often more materials to remove. If moisture lingered or dark spots appear, mold remediation is handled before walls are closed up.

You do not have to pick the exact service before you call. The dispatcher asks what you see, where the water came from and how long ago, then explains where to begin.

Each service page lists the equipment used, the factors that change cost and the questions homeowners ask most often. None of them quotes a flat price, because the scope depends on the water, the materials and the area.

What We Do Not Do

Restoration is not waterproofing, plumbing repair or structural engineering. If water keeps coming into a lower level, a drainage contractor is the right call. If a pipe needs replacing, a plumber does that while the restoration crew dries the structure.

We also do not decide insurance coverage. We document the loss, and your insurer decides what the policy covers.

Questions about any of this are welcome on the phone, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly if a job is better handled by another trade.
