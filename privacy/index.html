Privacy Policy

This site is call-only. Here is what that means for your information.

Information We Collect

This website has no forms. When you call, we collect what you tell the dispatcher, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the damage, so a crew can be sent.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How We Use It

We use call information to dispatch a crew, explain the scope and price, follow up on the job and improve our service. We do not use it for anything unrelated.

Our website host may log standard technical data such as page requests and browser type to keep the site working.

Sharing

We share your details with the restoration professionals who perform your job and with service providers who help run our phone line. We share information when the law requires it.

We do not describe this website as selling your data.

Cookies and Third Parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map. Those services may set their own cookies or collect technical data under their own policies.

You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Your Choices and Contact

You can ask what we have on file or ask us to delete it by calling (714) 515-1308 or emailing contact@fountainvalleywaterdamagepros.com.

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page applies when you read it.
